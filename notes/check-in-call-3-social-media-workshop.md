## TL;DR

- **Account names must be `haven.<city>.hackclub`** (e.g. `haven.burlington.hackclub`), with strong Hack Club branding, **no AI-looking visuals**, **no claiming regions** (no `hackclub.india`), and **at least one Hack Club staff member (Lela or Cisco) as admin/recovery contact**.
- **Good promo = a hook in the first 1–2 seconds + knowing your local audience + clear info on how to sign up** (who/what/when/where/why — not in every post, but enough to know it's an event).
- Simple things work: an event-promo reel from existing photos got ~8,000 views. Collaborate across events, shitpost, post text carousels.
- **Funding (TBD at the time):** social accounts can earn **$25–$1,000** (around **25,000 views** across all posts/platforms). *(Later call 7 states $10 per 10,000 views.)* **Register your accounts with the QR/form.**
- Best time to post: unpredictable; try **midday/early afternoon (≈2–6 PM local) on weekdays**.

### [14:13–15:25] What makes good event promo
*(slide: "What makes good event promo?" — 1. Interesting HOOKS (sound effects, fast movement, text, interesting visually — background & cinematography); 2. Understanding your audience (local to YOU!); 3. Providing information & direction (tell them to sign up for Haven! And how to!))*
- **Hook:** an interesting first carousel slide / first few seconds of a reel — something that makes people want to learn more ("what's this cool thing in my area?") and then sign up for Haven.
- **Know your audience:** events are in a city you know — use local culture/specific things as a bridge to the game jam.
- **Give information:** even a viral video is useless if it doesn't tell people to come or link them in. Include who/what/when/where/why — not in every post, but enough that people understand it's an event.

### [15:25–16:55] Making engaging reels, and tools
- **The first two seconds of a reel matter most for engagement:** e.g. a zoom-in for the first shot or someone talking really fast; great cinematography; catchy music or sound effects.
- *(slide "I have no idea how to make videos — we gotchu…")*: **CapCut (free)**, **DaVinci Resolve (free + professional)** — used by the previous Hack Club videographer — and **Premiere (monthly cost, but Cisco and Lela's preferred app)**. Tutorial videos are linked on the slide; ask Lela/Cisco about Premiere. [The audio between ≈15:46 and 16:16 is missing from the transcript.]
- For **carousel posts**, **Canva** ("canvas" in the transcript) is free and "really good".

### [16:55–18:55] Examples of good promo
- *(slide "Example of good promo")* **Sunbeam Karachi** built an account from scratch purely for event promo; an Instagram reel there got **~8,000 views** — "really good for a reel off an account just built for an event". Simple, fun, made by gathering photos — if you've been to past events, use your camera roll. The slide also shows the reel's collaborator accounts (collab posts).
- *(slide "Or complicated like this one.")* A more produced example — a **b-roll with a joke or pun**, a style used a lot on the main Hack Club page — can be tailored to your event.
- **Talking-head** videos (e.g. the Hack Club video "Are you a teenager that likes to make stuff? Here's how you can get up to a thousand dollars for your next project"): talk straight to the camera with a clear linear progression — what the event is, what they did there, what Hack Club is. Possible for Haven videos but "a lot more difficult — a lot of nuance"; good diction/pronunciation matters.
